# Visual Legal AID Explainer

## 📚 Overview

**Visual Legal AID Explainer** is a web application designed to simplify complex Indian laws and legal documents for common people. It provides explanations in English, Hindi, and Marathi, making legal information accessible to everyone.

## ✨ Features

### 1. **Document Analysis**
   - Upload or paste legal documents
   - Get instant simplifications in 3 languages
   - Automatic detection of legal sections
   - Key points extraction
   - Suggested next steps

### 2. **Legal Section Search**
   - Search for any Indian law or section
   - Includes: IPC, CrPC, Consumer Protection Act, POCSO, etc.
   - Detailed explanations with real-life examples
   - Information about when law applies and punishments

### 3. **FAQ Chatbot**
   - Ask legal questions in simple language
   - Get step-by-step guidance
   - Relevant helpline numbers provided
   - Quick access buttons for common topics

### 4. **Emergency Helplines**
   - Quick access to important contact numbers
   - Organized by category
   - Emergency and legal support numbers

## 🎯 Key Benefits

✅ **No Legal Jargon** - Explanations in very simple language
✅ **Multilingual** - English, Hindi, and Marathi
✅ **Free & Accessible** - No registration required
✅ **Informative** - Educational content, not legal advice
✅ **Mobile Friendly** - Works on all devices

## 🚀 How to Use

### For Document Analysis:
1. Go to "Upload Document" section
2. Paste your legal text
3. Click "Analyze Document"
4. Get simplification in all 3 languages
5. See detected sections and next steps

### For Searching Laws:
1. Go to "Search Laws" section
2. Type law name or section (e.g., "IPC 420", "Consumer Rights")
3. Select from suggestions
4. Get complete explanation with examples

### For FAQ:
1. Go to "FAQ" section
2. Ask your question
3. Get answer with relevant helplines
4. Or use quick buttons for common topics
